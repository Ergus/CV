\section{Summary}

Senior software developer with a PhD in Computer Architecture and High
Performance Computing (HPC), and over 12 years of expertise in
designing, developing, and optimizing hybrid parallel applications for
a range of architectures, languages, and infrastructures. Key
contributions include developing the task-based programming model
\href{https://github.com/bsc-pm/ompss-2-cluster-releases}{OmpSs2@Cluster}
and the instrumentation-based profiler
\href{https://github.com/Ergus/ExtraeWin}{ExtraeWin} for C++ parallel
applications. Actively involved in open-source development as a
regular contributor to the Emacs Project and maintainer of several
packages, including
\href{https://elpa.gnu.org/packages/gtags-mode.html}{gtags-mode}.
